## Content Risks

### Risk 1: False Claims About Logo or Photography

**Status:** HIGH RISK

**What to avoid:**
- "Designed the Sakisa Seafoods logo" ✗
- "Photographed the food" ✗
- "Curated the photography" ✗

**Safeguard:** Explicitly attribute these to client in case study.

**Mitigation:** Evidence audit clearly documents these are client-provided. Cross-check every claim.

---

### Risk 2: Overstating Google Sites Capabilities

**Status:** MEDIUM RISK

**What to avoid:**
- "Built a custom responsive website" ✗
- "Implemented custom code" ✗
- "Designed for mobile-first development" ✗
- Specific technical claims about Google Sites that aren't evidenced

**Safeguard:** Use careful language: "implemented within Google Sites", "worked within platform capabilities"

**Mitigation:** Keep technical discussion minimal. Focus on design decisions, not technical implementation details.

**Status:** HIGH RISK

- "Increased orders by X%" ✗

- "Website traffic improved" ✗

### Risk 4: Claiming Design Process That Isn't Evidenced

- "Usability testing validated..." ✗
- "Customer interviews revealed..." ✗
- "Market research demonstrated..." ✗

**Mitigation:** Use observable language ("The design includes", "The layout features") rather than process language ("Research showed").

### Risk 5: Overstating Contribution

**Status:** MEDIUM RISK

### Risk 6: AI Content Creation Overly Prominent

**Status:** LOW RISK (but worth managing)
