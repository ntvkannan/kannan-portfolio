# Yoga Center — Evidence Audit

## Purpose

This document inventories and classifies all evidence from the Yoga Center (Nagendran Yoga International) project. The goal is to understand exactly what work was completed, what can be claimed, and what narrative structure best represents the actual design work.

This is a **brand identity and visual communication project**, not a UX/product case study like School ERP or Kit Management.

**Evidence Classification:**
- **CONFIRMED** — Direct evidence from source material; clear proof of work
- **PROBABLE** — Visual evidence suggests likelihood, but not explicit
- **UNCLEAR** — Evidence exists but purpose/scope is ambiguous
- **NOT FOUND** — No evidence in available material

## 2. Design System Analysis

### Color Palette

**Primary Color:**
- Bright Orange (#FF9933 or similar) — Dominant across 95%+ of all materials
- Used for: backgrounds, logo, typography, accents, UI elements

**Secondary Colors:**
- Deep Maroon/Burgundy (appears on some posters like Now-Open and RYS)
- Cream/Off-white (backgrounds, text on orange)
- White (text, accents, logo strokes)

**Accent Colors (used in photography/illustrations):**
- Yellow-green (course 300 card, RYS sunset)
- Pink/Magenta (Aerial Yoga photo overlay)
- Blue (Aerial Yoga instructor clothes)
- These appear contextual to content, not core brand palette

**Assessment:** Strong, consistent color system. Orange dominance creates immediate recognition. Secondary maroon suggests variation for special announcements or certificates.

### Typography

**Headlines:**
- Bold, all-caps or title case
- Sans-serif appearance (exact font unclear)
- Italic style used in some social posts (Hatha Yoga 5am, others)
- Size hierarchy clear

**Body Text:**
- Sans-serif (consistent with headlines)
- Regular weight for descriptions
- White text on orange common pattern
- Website text appears to mix serif and sans-serif

**Assessment:** Consistent type system. No clear evidence of formal font specifications documented, but visual consistency suggests deliberate choices.

### Graphic Language

**Recurring Elements:**
- Yoga pose silhouettes (seated, standing, aerial)
- Mandala patterns (ornate circular geometric)
- Wing motifs (part of logo, possibly elsewhere)
- Sun/circular shapes (representing energy, enlightenment)
- Decorative borders and frames
- Ornamental separators (stars, dots, lines)

**Photography Style:**
- Action/practice photography (instructors, students)
- Outdoor nature scenes (trees, gardens, sunrise/sunset)
- Curated to show community and practice

**Assessment:** Cohesive graphic language. Mixing of illustration, photography, and geometric elements creates balanced visual system. Heavy use of yoga-specific iconography (silhouettes, poses, mandala).
